MobBuckets is a fun experience to play around with since the whole premiss of the project is to mimic the mechanics of fish buckets but for any mob. Yes, that includes bosses such as the Wither or Ender Dragon.
![Features section divider](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/fec245585cf59a9d26b364fa4791478b5008b942.png)
- Fabric and Paper Support _(Mod version is heavily advised as it will be a more stable enviornment)_
- Mod is both **Client** and **Server,** meaning if the server has it installed, you don't need the mod installed on your end.
- **Shift+Right-Click** while holding an empty bucket to capture a mob. (Doesn't work on anything you can capture in a water bucket: Fish, Axolotl, Tadpoles.)
- The ability to **clutch** with any mob as long as you release the mob and collide with their hitbox before you hit the ground! (Works with the "Bucket of Player" too!)
- **Multi-player "Bucket of Player" crafting** allows you to craft a "Bucket of Player" with up to 8 different player's playerheads at once!
- The "Bucket of Player" **teleports** the **bound players** to where you release them. (Useful for saving or trapping or clutching on them to kill them >:D)
- **Data persistence** for mobs, such as sheeps or villagers _(Don't worry about trades resetting!)_
- **Players drop their playerheads when they die,** these can be used to bind a player to a "Bucket of Player" when crafting a playerhead with a bucket.
